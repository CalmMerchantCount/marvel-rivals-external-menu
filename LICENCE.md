## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Practice Layout Notes — study hero rotations from any checkpoint
- Range Visualizer — highlight practice targets and ability ranges
- Replay Notes — record and review practice runs
- Playback Speed — adjust practice playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Hero Catalog — browse heroes, outfits, colors, effects
- Custom Skin Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Ability Trail Notes — improved visibility of ability effects

### Aimbot
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any map layout
- Movement Notes — manually review jumps and dashes mid-run
- Chain Notes — study complex maneuvers in practice
